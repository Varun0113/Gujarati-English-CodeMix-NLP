RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

Experimental Workflow

A principled approach to handling code-mixed Gujarati-English data.

COLLECT

Curated bilingual and code-mixed conversational corpora covering academic, entertainment, and social domains.

CLEAN

Regex tokenization preserving native Gujarati Unicode (`\u0A80`–`\u0AFF`), Latin characters, and punctuation.

NORMALIZE

Phonetic reduction for lengthened characters and Romanized dictionary canonicalization.

REPRESENT

Sublinear TF-IDF word and character n-grams to mitigate out-of-vocabulary sparsity.

CLASSIFY

Calibrated Logistic Regression with L2 regularization to produce smooth probability distributions.

EVALUATE

Stratified holdout evaluation reporting Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and weighted F1-Scores.

Current Research Limitations

  • Prototype-sized dataset: The models are trained on a curated corpus of 115 sentiment and 126 topic samples designed for academic demonstration.
  • Spelling diversity: Romanized Gujarati has highly variable informal spelling without standardized orthography; unmapped tokens are tagged as Unknown.
  • Classical linear ML bounds: TF-IDF representations capture lexical surface n-grams without deep transformer-level self-attention.
  • Topic classifier bounds: The 8-class topic model achieves 57.69% accuracy; predictions are statistical estimates rather than guarantees.
  • Confidence diagnostics: Individual prediction confidence represents calibrated softmax probabilities for the given text.
